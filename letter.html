A letter for your birthday
TO MY FAVOURITE PERSON13 · 10 · 2026

My Monica,

Happy birthday, my love.

It's still a little wild to me that I stumbled upon you and found someone who can be my best friend, my peace, my excitement and the person who makes me want to keep growing. You are strict and soft, mature and full of life, and I hope you never lose the things that make you so wonderfully you.

When you told me, “I am your aunt now,” after finding out my age, I genuinely wondered whether I was about to lose my chance at travelling and enjoying wine with my aunt. Your “No lol” didn't exactly reassure me. And now look at us. You're not my aunt; you're my best mate, my woman, and someone I want beside me for the long road ahead.

I hope this next year brings you closer to the life you dream of. I hope we get to witness it, experience it and live it together. May we keep choosing each other, growing beside each other, and finding new reasons to be grateful we crossed paths.

And most importantly, may your cocktail always be beside my beer in this long life ahead of us. I want to savour it all with you: the big adventures, the small rituals, the good days, and even the days that ask a little more of us.

Happy birthday, Monica. I'm glad it's you.

Always yours,

Godknows ♡
